About Us LANG LIEU


HOANG VAN THANH (Thanh Bin)

Lang Lieu is a short-form video channel created by HOANG VAN THANH (Thanh Bin), known for his simple style, often wearing a white T-shirt and black shorts. He lives in a rural mountainous area in Northern Vietnam.
The channel focuses on rural food, unique challenges, and viral entertainment content, featuring participation from his brothers, friends, and local people in the village, and has gained billions of views across social platforms.
